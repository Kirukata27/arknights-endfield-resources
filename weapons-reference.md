## How Weapons Work

Each operator equips one weapon that matches their weapon type. Weapons provide:
- **ATK stat scaling** that grows with weapon level
- **Passive Boost** — a persistent effect active in and out of battle
- **Active Boost** — an effect triggered by specific actions (Combo Skill, Skill use, etc.)

Weapons are leveled via **Tuning** — collecting materials and spending them through the weapon's tuning tree.

## Weapon Farming (Arsenal Exchange)

Arsenal Exchange is the gacha pool for weapons. Key facts:
- **Separate pity from character banners** — always
- Hard pity at **pull 40** (any 6★, 25% featured) and **pull 80** (guaranteed featured)
- Every 10 pulls: guaranteed 5★ or higher
- Pity **resets each new Arsenal Exchange issue**
- **Arms Offering milestones are cumulative**: every 100 → 180 → 260… pulls total across ALL issues gives bonus rewards (alternating between standard selector and exclusive featured weapon)

> Track your Arsenal Exchange pity: [reend.vallov.com/headhunt-tracker](https://reend.vallov.com/headhunt-tracker/)

## Weapon Build Priority

For most players:
1. Get the **signature weapon** for your main DPS (T0/T0.5 operators)
2. Use **5★ alternatives** for supports and secondary DPS
3. Level the weapon to max — weapon level has the highest ATK return
4. Invest in **Tuning** to unlock passive bonuses

**Do not** spread tuning resources across too many weapons. Focus one weapon at a time.
