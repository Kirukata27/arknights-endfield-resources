# Arknights: Endfield — Team Building Guide

Building an effective team in Endfield goes beyond just picking your highest-rarity operators. This guide covers the core mechanics that determine why certain team compositions consistently outperform others.

---

## The 4-Operator Meta

The current standard endgame composition runs **4 operators simultaneously**:

```
[Main DPS] + [Sub DPS / Amplifier] + [Supporter] + [Defender]
```

Each role has a specific job, and substituting roles without understanding the mechanic can collapse your damage output significantly.

---

## Understanding Vulnerable

**Vulnerable** is the most important debuff in Endfield. It amplifies all incoming damage to the affected enemy. Most Supporters and some Defenders apply it as part of their kit.

**The rotation principle:**
1. Supporter applies Vulnerable
2. DPS deals damage during the Vulnerable window
3. Repeat

If your DPS acts *before* Vulnerable is applied, you waste a significant portion of your theoretical damage. This is why Supporter placement in the action queue matters.

**Operators that apply Vulnerable efficiently:**
- Endministrator (on Combo Skill)
- Lifeng (passive)
- Tangtang (Ultimate)
- Ember (Combo Skill, as a Defender)

---

## Combo Skill Chains

Combo Skills activate after using a standard Skill. The order in which operators use their skills creates a **chain** — and the sequence affects which Combo Skills fire.

**Example chain (Chen Qianyu team):**
```
Chen QY Skill → Chen QY Combo Skill (Lightning burst)
↓ triggers
Zhuang Fangyi Combo Skill (team buff)
↓ triggers
Sub DPS Combo Skill
```

Understanding your team's specific combo chain is one of the biggest skill-expression points in Endfield. For operator-specific chains: [reend.vallov.com/characters](https://reend.vallov.com/characters/)

## Recommended Teams by Playstyle

### Hyper DPS (Speed Clear)
**Laevatain / Lifeng / Endministrator / Ember**
- Laevatain + Lifeng for constant Heat application
- Endministrator applies Vulnerable via Combo
- Ember sustains and reapplies Vulnerable
- High skill ceiling, massive payoff when rotations are clean

### Balanced (General Content)
**Chen Qianyu / Zhuang Fangyi / Perlica / Last Rite**
- Strong sustained damage with Electric synergy
- Perlica provides Cryo for Freeze potential
- Last Rite covers Physical damage gap

### Beginner Friendly
**Pogranichnik / Tangtang / Fluorite / Snowshine**
- All available on standard banner
- Forgiving rotation — Tangtang Vulnerable is easy to apply
- Snowshine provides reliable sustain

### Budget (4★/5★ only)
**Da Pan / Ardelia / Akekuri / Alesh**
- Da Pan is a solid Electric DPS at 5★
- Alesh handles AoE Cryo
- Akekuri applies minor Vulnerable through support
- Viable for most story content

## Gear Set Priority

Gear sets are one of the biggest power multipliers in Endfield. A well-geared 5★ often outperforms a poorly-geared 6★.

| Gear Set | Best For | Effect (2-piece) |
|----------|----------|-----------------|
| **LYNX** | Defenders, Healers | HP / Healing boost |
| **Crimson Ring** | Heat DPS | ATK + Heat dmg boost |
| **Cascade** | Cryo / Freeze comps | Cryo dmg + Freeze duration |
| **Thunderhead** | Electric DPS | Electric dmg + chain lightning |
| **Ironclad** | Physical tanks | DEF + Physical resistance |

**Priority rule:** Always go for a **4-piece set bonus** on your main DPS. Mix 2-piece on supports if full sets aren't available yet.

## Common Mistakes

**1. Ignoring Vulnerable timing**
Applying damage before Vulnerable is active is the #1 DPS loss. Delay your DPS actions by 0.5–1s after Supporter skill lands.

**2. Leveling the wrong skills**
Combo Skill is almost always the highest priority. Battle Skill (the one with a longer cooldown) is often the weakest — level it last.

**3. Skipping gear**
Even a 2-piece set bonus is a significant damage increase. Prioritize farming gear over pulling more operators.

**4. Not using Potential Tokens**
Potential upgrades at P4 and P6 are often game-changing. Use your tokens — don't hoard them.

**5. Building too many operators**
Focus resources on 4 operators max in early game. Spreading upgrades thin delays all your characters.
